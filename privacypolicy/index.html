Privacy Policy

LoopKO · Last updated 16 October 2026
The short version: LoopKO is supported by advertising. You don't need an account to play, and we don't directly sell your personal information. Advertising and analytics partners may process device information and advertising identifiers, as described below.

This Privacy Policy explains how LoopKO (“LoopKO”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the LoopKO mobile game (the “Game”) on iOS or Android.

By using LoopKO, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

LoopKO may collect certain information automatically when you use the Game.

Depending on your device, operating system, location, consent choices, and the services enabled in the Game, this may include:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system and version
  • Game version
  • Language and country or region
  • IP address
  • Advertising identifiers, where permitted
  • App or installation identifiers
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Network and connection information
  • Technical and diagnostic information
  • Crash and performance information
  • Gameplay and usage information
  • Advertising interaction information

LoopKO does not require you to create an account in order to play the Game.

We do not intentionally request your name, postal address, telephone number, or email address simply to play LoopKO.

2. Gameplay and Analytics

LoopKO uses third-party analytics technology to understand how players use the Game and to improve its performance and gameplay.

Analytics information may include:

  • Game sessions
  • Gameplay events
  • Scores and progression
  • Features used
  • Game performance
  • Crash and diagnostic information
  • Device and technical information
  • Other pseudonymous or aggregated gameplay information

We use analytics information to:

  • Improve gameplay
  • Understand how features are used
  • Identify bugs and technical problems
  • Improve performance and stability
  • Analyze aggregate gameplay patterns
  • Develop and improve future features

Analytics providers may process this information on our behalf.

3. Advertising

LoopKO is supported by advertising.

We may use third-party advertising providers and advertising mediation services to display, deliver, measure, and optimize advertisements in the Game.

These services may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Approximate location
  • Game or app information
  • Advertising interactions
  • Technical information
  • Information used to determine whether an advertisement was displayed or interacted with

Depending on your location, device settings, consent choices, and applicable law, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

We do not directly sell your personal information to advertisers.

Advertising providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

4. Third-Party Services

LoopKO uses third-party service providers to operate, analyze, and monetize the Game.

These may include providers in the following categories:

  • Advertising networks
  • Advertising mediation platforms
  • Analytics providers
  • Crash and performance services
  • App platform services
  • Other technical service providers necessary to operate the Game

These providers may receive or collect information through software development kits (SDKs) or other technologies integrated into LoopKO.

We select and configure these services in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and contractual obligations.

The specific third-party services used by LoopKO may change over time.

5. iOS Tracking and Advertising

On Apple devices, LoopKO may request permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework where required.

Tracking may involve linking information from LoopKO with information from other companies’ apps, websites, or services for purposes such as targeted advertising or advertising measurement.

If permission is required and you do not grant it, LoopKO will not perform tracking covered by Apple’s App Tracking Transparency requirements.

You can change your tracking preferences at any time through your device settings.

Apple explains that developers are responsible for the data collection and tracking practices of third-party SDKs included in their apps.

6. Privacy Choices and Consent

Depending on your location and applicable laws, LoopKO may display a privacy or consent notice before certain information is collected or used.

Your choices may affect:

  • Personalized advertising
  • Analytics
  • Advertising measurement
  • Certain features that depend on data processing

Where consent is legally required, we will request consent before performing the applicable processing.

You may also have additional privacy choices provided by your operating system, browser, or applicable law.

7. Children’s Privacy

LoopKO is not specifically directed at children unless the Game is expressly identified as being directed toward children or included in a child-directed category.

We do not intentionally request children to provide their name, email address, telephone number, or other directly identifying information simply to play the Game.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information through LoopKO, please contact us using the contact information below.

If LoopKO is marketed or configured as a child-directed or family-targeted application, additional requirements may apply to advertising, analytics, consent, and data collection.

8. How We Use Information

Information collected through LoopKO may be used to:

  • Provide and operate the Game
  • Deliver advertisements
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Provide analytics
  • Understand gameplay
  • Improve gameplay and features
  • Detect and fix bugs
  • Monitor performance and stability
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity
  • Maintain security
  • Comply with applicable laws
  • Respond to privacy requests

We may share information with third-party service providers that help us operate LoopKO, including providers of:

  • Advertising

  • Crash reporting

  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Other services necessary to operate and improve the Game

We may also disclose information where required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of LoopKO, its users, or others.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including operating the Game, providing analytics and advertising, maintaining security, complying with legal obligations, and resolving disputes.
