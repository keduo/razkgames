Privacy Policy

LoopKO & razkgames.com · Last updated 15 September 2026
The short version: LoopKO works offline, we don't ask you to make an account, and we don't sell your data. What follows is the detail.

What we collect

Your scores, settings, and progress are stored on your own device. If you enable iCloud or Google Play backup, your operating system may copy that save file to your account — we never see it.

We receive anonymous, aggregated diagnostics from the app stores and our crash reporter: device model, OS version, app version, and crash traces. These contain no names, emails, or advertising identifiers.

Children

LoopKO is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. There is no chat, no user profiles, and no behavioural advertising in the game.

Advertising and purchases

LoopKO does not contain third-party ad networks. If we add optional in-app purchases, payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google — we never receive your card details, only a confirmation that a purchase succeeded.

Service providers

We rely on a small number of processors to run the studio: the Apple App Store and Google Play for distribution and crash reporting, and an email provider for support correspondence. Each handles data under its own privacy terms and only for the purposes above.

Your rights

You may ask us what we hold about you, request a copy, or ask us to delete it. Email privacy@razkgames.com and we will respond within 30 days. To erase all local game data yourself, delete the app from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and note the change in the app's release notes.
