Support

Stuck, crashed, or lost your streak? Start here — and if none of this helps, email support@razkgames.com.

Is LoopKO out yet?
Yes! LoopKO is out now on the App Store for iPhone. The Android version is coming to Google Play soon.
Does it need an internet connection?
No. LoopKO is fully playable offline. A connection is only used if you choose to share a score.
I lost my progress after reinstalling.
Progress lives in your device's app backup. Restoring your phone from iCloud or Google Play backup brings it back; a fresh install on a new device starts clean. If a score vanished without a reinstall, email us the approximate date and your device model.
The game crashes or freezes on launch.
Restart your device, make sure the app is on the latest version, and confirm you have at least 200 MB of free storage. If it still crashes, send us your device model and OS version and we'll investigate directly.
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No ad networks and no paywalls. If we ever add an optional purchase, it will be a one-time unlock, never a timer or an energy system.
Can I make a video or stream LoopKO?
Yes, always — monetised or not. You don't need our permission. Email press@razkgames.com if you want early access or press assets.
Reporting a bug?
Include your device model, OS version, and what you were doing when it happened. Screenshots or a screen recording get things fixed roughly twice as fast.
